## Direct answer

This is now a genuinely strong, varied and publicly attractive technology × place slate. It is not yet honest to call all fourteen equally secure or definitively the most compelling possible fourteen.

- **Slots 2–9, 12 and 14 earn clear editorial seats** subject to ordinary evidence closure.
- **Slot 1 earns a protected conditional seat** because patients and early medical infrastructure supply history and human stakes absent elsewhere, but it fails if contemporaneous system artifacts and patient/worker experience remain abstract.
- **Slots 10 and 11 both remain only under strict Las Vegas Paiute/community-authority and nonduplication gates.** Their cultural importance is high; state records cannot earn either seat on the Tribe's behalf.
- **Slot 13 is the weakest incumbent.** Its $128 million cross-model disagreement is intellectually strong, but opaque external methods, conflicting local survey counts and limited human immediacy make it the only selected story that must defeat audited challengers before the slate locks.

No alternate is promoted today because none has received the same focused four-lens audit required of incumbents. Automatic promotion would repeat the selection error this process was built to correct.

## Scoring method

Each proposition was rescored from 1–5 on eight dimensions after its audit:

1. immediate public appeal;
2. cultural/public consequence;
3. Nevada/place specificity;
4. central and tangible technology;
5. people and labor;
6. evidence recoverability;
7. real visual/video potential;
8. distinctiveness within this fourteen.

Scores prioritize the proposition that can actually be produced, not merely the best theoretical headline. A high total does not override an authority, ethics, causation or evidence hard gate.

## Editorial tiers

### Tier A — flagship anchors

**Slots 5, 6, 8, 9, 12 and 14.** These combine an irresistible premise, visible mechanism, Nevada consequence, human labor and strong evidence/visual routes. They should carry the series launch and promotional burden.

### Tier B — essential range and explanation

**Slots 2, 3, 4 and 7.** These make the underlying systems understandable and give readers usable objects: tag/data representation, public catalog, laboratory “pass,” and kief-enhanced flower. They remain distinct only if their boundaries below are enforced.

### Tier C — culturally necessary but authority/evidence gated

**Slots 1, 10 and 11.** These prevent the project from becoming a contemporary state-regulator/product catalogue with patients and tribal governments pushed to the margins. Their inclusion cannot justify thin evidence or speaking for affected communities.

### Tier D — challenger seat

**Slot 13.** Preserve its completed audit, but do not lock it or spend article-drafting resources until challenger audits are complete.

1. **Slot 13 is not locked.** Audit the ID-scanner and child-resistant-package challengers before dossier work begins on Slot 13.

4. **Slot 3 remains only if** the preserved 28-attempt catalog audit produces consequential, reproducible findings. If not, compare the laboratory-sample challenger.
5. **Slot 10 automatically exits if** Las Vegas Paiute governmental/cannabis authority, meaningful review opportunity or culturally lawful reporting/visual conditions cannot be obtained.
6. **Slot 11 automatically exits if** no nation-specific, transaction-level implementation can be reconstructed or if its treatment duplicates Slot 10.
7. Failure of Slots 10 or 11 does not authorize collapsing tribal sovereignty into a state-only explanation. Replace the failed story and preserve any community-authorized material in the surviving one.
8. No score authorizes drafting while an itemized evidence, authority, legal, scientific, privacy, rights, video, accessibility or review gate remains open.
