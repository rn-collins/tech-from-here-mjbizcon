## Evidentiary rules

- `V` = verified by a located primary or official source.
- `Q` = verified only with the stated qualification.
- `O` = open; do not draft as fact.
- `R` = external technical, scientific, legal, or affected-authority review remains.
- A regulator's report establishes its own methods, findings, and actions—not independent proof that the regulated system worked.
- Dynamic interfaces and current licensing facts require a dated recapture immediately before publication.

**Bounded thesis:** Nevada constitutionalized a legislatively implemented medical-cannabis system years before it created the later state-regulated dispensary framework. The gap is between legal recognition, administrative registration, and practical supply—not a claim that every patient lacked all lawful means of access.
