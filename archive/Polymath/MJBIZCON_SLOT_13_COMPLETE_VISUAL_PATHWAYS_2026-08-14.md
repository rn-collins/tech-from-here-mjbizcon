## 4. Strongest single-image decision

**Winner if captured and reconciled:** RN Frame 8, a sharp macro of one actual Nevada-marked serving, with a small scale beside it and the complete corresponding package softly visible behind it.

Why it wins:

- it makes the technology immediately legible at phone size;
- it is specific to Nevada and to one documented product;
- it carries the story's surprising claim without decorative design;
- RN controls the copyright; and
- it can be paired with a caption that separates the mark from dose, package approval and effectiveness.

**Required caption:**

> A serving from **[exact product]**, purchased by RN at **[exact licensed retailer]** on **[date]**, bears Nevada's universal cannabis symbol. The mark signals that the serving contains cannabis; this photograph alone does not establish dose accuracy, regulatory approval, consumer recognition or the equipment that made it. Photograph by RN.

**Required alt text:** describe the serving's color, form, scale, raised/printed mark and the package relationship in plain language; spell out the letters T-H-C. Do not begin with “image of,” and do not encode conclusions the photograph cannot prove.

If the item-level chain fails, this image is not eligible. The next fallback is the official symbol diagram after rights review; the final fallback is RN's exact-retailer exterior with a provenance-limited caption.
