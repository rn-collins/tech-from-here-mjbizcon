## Review Lane 1 — Nevada law and regulatory process

### Questions RN must decide

1. Does every sentence distinguish statute, regulation, guidance, form, proposal, adoption and implementation?
2. Does the chronology correctly separate 2017 proposal, 2018 effectiveness, 2019 compliance, 2021 terminology standardization and current continuation?
3. Does the article avoid converting an exception request into automatic approval?
4. Does it avoid treating package approval as proof of every produced serving's compliance?
5. Does it state the R152-24 posture accurately on publication day?

### Supported now

- The serving-level mechanism originated in R092-17 and became effective 27 February 2018, with a 1 January 2019 compliance deadline reflected in CCB guidance.
- Qualifying servings must carry the Board-approved Nevada universal cannabis symbol.
- The symbol may not be modified; milligrams may be placed beside, not within, it under the official guidance.
- Impractical products use a Board-administered exemption/review path supported by product description and photographs; approvals are retained at the production facility for inspection.
- The 2021 amendment formalized the term “Nevada universal cannabis symbol”; it did not create the serving-level requirement.

### Blocked language

- “Every Nevada edible bears the symbol.”
- “The state approves every serving.”
- “An exemption means the product carries no cannabis indicator.”
- “R152-24 is effective” unless the final record proves it.
- “Nevada was first,” “Nevada is the only state,” or equivalent priority claims.
