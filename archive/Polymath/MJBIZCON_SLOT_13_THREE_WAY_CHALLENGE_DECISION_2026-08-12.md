# MJBizCon Canonical 14 — Slot 13 Three-Way Challenge Decision

**Decision date:** 12 August 2026  
**Candidates compared:** illicit-market estimation; dispensary ID scanner; child-resistant cannabis package  
**Controlling decision:** **REPLACE THE ILLICIT-MARKET-ESTIMATION STORY WITH THE CHILD-RESISTANT-PACKAGE STORY. RETAIN THE ID SCANNER AS THE FIRST PROMOTION-READY ALTERNATE.**

## Outcome

The new provisional Slot 13 is:

**Nevada Calls This Cannabis Package Child-Resistant. The Claim Begins With a Stopwatch.**

Working narrative proposition: **A child-resistant cannabis package is a tested negotiation between a young child and an older hand. Nevada imports that human-performance standard into a retail object, then adds opacity, repeated-opening, approval and storage controls that must not be mistaken for childproofing.**

This is a seat-selection decision, not permission to draft or publish. Slot 13 remains blocked until one actual Nevada-approved package corresponds to its federal protocol report, approval record, production specification and real retail/reclosure workflow.

## Why this wins

The package candidate is the strongest combination of public magnetism, cultural consequence, concrete technology, people, visual evidence and slate distinctiveness. Its central surprise is immediately understandable but not trivial: the safety claim on an ordinary object comes from a standardized timed interaction with children aged 42–51 months and an adult-usability test involving adults aged 50–70. That mechanism naturally joins child safety, aging, disability/access, materials, manufacturing, retail labor and household practice.

Nevada specificity is real rather than decorative. NAC 678B.596 imports 16 C.F.R. Part 1700, demands opacity and continued effectiveness across multiple openings, while CCB pre-approval and the separate Nevada rules for warnings, child-directed imagery, serving controls and locking storage create a state-specific control stack. The story can show exactly where federal test architecture ends and Nevada implementation begins.

The candidate also improves the balance of the Canonical 14. The slate already contains several database, inference, tracking, measurement and surveillance systems. A closure mechanism and its human-performance proof add physical product design, polymer/closure engineering, factory quality labor, aging/accessibility and family safety without duplicating another slot's narrative job.

## Why the ID scanner does not win

The ID scanner is more Nevada-exclusive and remains an excellent story. But its most culturally magnetic question—what happens to the captured data—cannot be answered from the state's feature list. “Data and image capture” does not prove retention, transmission, loyalty linkage, facial recognition or police access. A responsible article therefore needs an approved model, vendor/facility configuration, privacy contract/policy, deletion and access path, plus a security-safe worker workflow. That burden is solvable but substantially more closed and privacy-sensitive.

The scanner also sits closer to Slot 1's identity system and Slot 12's surveillance/data-access concerns. Its distinct front-door authentication mechanism is sufficient to keep it as the first alternate, but not enough to displace the package's stronger slate-balancing value.

**Automatic promotion route:** if the package fails its object-to-test-to-approval correspondence gate and the scanner obtains an approved device, complete Nevada workflow and verified capture/retention/deletion/access map, promote the scanner without reopening the illicit-estimation story.

## Why the illicit-market estimate loses the seat

The estimation candidate is intellectually serious, but it is the least attractive and least recoverable of the three. Its official report turns a $242 million Equio projection and a separate $370 million RDCOS-derived scenario—using different source years and methods—into endpoints readers can easily mistake for one range. The report also contains an unresolved 114-versus-166 Nevada response count discrepancy. Essential model inputs, weighting, definitions, tourism treatment, uncertainty and calculation workbooks are not yet public in the recovered record.

Those defects could become the story, but the Canonical 14 already has multiple systems about measurement, databases, proxies and institutional knowledge. The candidate would add more abstract epistemology while the package adds a visible physical object and a wider public constituency. Preserve the estimation audit for a separate data/policy lane or revive it only if the underlying methods become open enough to reproduce.

## New Slot 13 ownership boundary

Slot 13 owns **the performance claim embedded in cannabis packaging**:

- closure geometry and materials;
- child and adult human-use testing;
- package/report/version correspondence;
- Nevada packaging approval;
- production and retail closure labor;
- repeated opening/resecuring;
- adult accessibility; and
- the boundary between package resistance and household storage.

It does not own Metrc package identity (Slot 2), cannabis analytical testing/COAs (Slots 3–5), recall traceability (Slot 6), waste processing (Slot 8), or broad public-health outcome claims. It may use poisoning data only if denominators, time, product types, exposure and competing causes support the exact claim.

## Hard gates before dossier or prose

1. Identify one current CCB-approved package and its exact manufacturer, model, size, materials, closure, instructions and version.
2. Match that object to the complete 16 C.F.R. § 1700.20 protocol report; verify configuration, conditioning, test type, failure definition and repeated-opening assumptions.
3. Obtain the Nevada submission/approval record or an authoritative equivalent and establish what CCB reviewed versus what the supplier tested.
4. Recover a real component-receipt → line setup → fill/seal/closure → inspection/release → retail handoff → consumer reclosure workflow.
5. Verify Nevada's operational meaning and evidence requirement for maintaining effectiveness across multiple openings.
6. Document adult accessibility with consent and without simulated disability claims; RN remains the sole final reviewer.
7. Build the real-video, real-image carousel, best-image, precise RN location assignment, LinkedIn and complete audio-edition package specified in the challenger audit.
8. Never test or reenact cannabis packaging with children; any protocol footage must be lawful, ethical, rights-cleared and use inert/surrogate contents.

## Automatic replacement triggers

Replace Slot 13 with the ID scanner if any of the following remains unresolved after one bounded evidence-closure cycle:

- no actual approved Nevada package can be identified;
- the package cannot be matched to a complete protocol report and approval record;
- material differences exist between the tested and retail configuration and cannot be resolved;
- “multiple openings” compliance cannot be evidenced honestly;
- no meaningful real visual or lawful in-feed route survives rights review; or
- accessibility reporting cannot be completed without unsupported generalization.

Do not reinstate illicit-market estimation merely because both physical candidates encounter access friction. Reconsider it only on newly recovered reproducible model evidence and a fresh full-slate comparison.

## Package-format inheritance

The promoted story inherits every existing Canonical 14 requirement: complete Beehiiv article and citations; claim and causal ledger; watched-video investigation; real-image carousel with an actual meaningful visual on every page; item-level provenance, rights, caption, credit and alt text; precise RN place-based photography assignment; best single image; independent LinkedIn post and first comment; RN's real/verified-AI/hybrid audio choice, listening-adapted script, spoken citations, pronunciation sheet, disclosure, filename/runtime/version/placement, written transcript route and complete audio QA; mobile/accessibility/release QA; and no publication, scheduling, sending or outreach without RN authorization.

## Final decision

**PROMOTE THE CHILD-RESISTANT-PACKAGE PROPOSITION TO PROVISIONAL SLOT 13. RETAIN THE ID SCANNER AS FIRST ALTERNATE. REMOVE ILLICIT-MARKET ESTIMATION FROM THE CANONICAL 14 AND PRESERVE ITS AUDIT AS A RESEARCH ASSET.**

Nothing in this decision authorizes article drafting, publication, scheduling, sending, purchasing, permission outreach, interviews, public-record requests, child testing, test purchases, field access or public uploading.
