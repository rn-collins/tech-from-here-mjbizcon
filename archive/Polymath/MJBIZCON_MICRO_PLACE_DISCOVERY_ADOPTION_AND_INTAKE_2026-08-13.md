## Controlling decision

The Micro-Place Discovery addition now governs this MJBizCon/Las Vegas production system as a separate content engine. It is additive. It does not change the requirement for exactly fourteen canonical articles and does not replace any article, LinkedIn, event-week, audio, evidence, visual, real-image, carousel, RN location-photography, rights, accessibility or release gate.

Small but magnetic objects and places must no longer be discarded merely because they cannot sustain a canonical article. They will be preserved as independent “Did you know?” posts, *Look Closer* discoveries, canonical preludes/companions/afterlives, route clusters, event-linked details, field videos, audio notes or newsletter interludes.

No discovery will be published merely because it is visually attractive. Every accepted record requires exact location, current state, mechanism, creator/labor, place connection, primary evidence, actual visual, rights state, access truth, accessibility treatment and a precise RN capture plan.

## Geographic truth and scope

“Las Vegas” is often used commercially for places outside the incorporated City of Las Vegas. Every record must distinguish:

- City of Las Vegas;
- unincorporated Paradise;
- unincorporated Winchester;
- Enterprise and Spring Valley where relevant;
- North Las Vegas;
- Henderson;
- Boulder City;
- Harry Reid International Airport and its jurisdictional truth;
- Las Vegas Convention Center and the precise campus/building/route;
- separately labeled Nevada excursions such as Hoover Dam or Tonopah; and
- remote statewide objects that are relevant but not reasonable RN field assignments.

A resort name, postal address or tourism label does not establish municipal location.

## Search languages and terminology

English is primary. Search variants must include object, infrastructure, labor and status terminology rather than only “hidden gems”: public art; kinetic; interactive; responsive; clock; time system; neon; sign; typeface; wayfinding; tunnel; people mover; monorail; loop; ventilation; cooling; flood channel; detention basin; pump; intake; substation; transmission; communications; antenna; benchmark; survey marker; atomic; testing; laboratory; casino technology; surveillance; ticketing; accessibility; adaptive reuse; industrial remnant; removed; relocated; restored; commissioned; fabricated; maintained; behind the scenes; worker; operator; and every zone/place name.

Spanish query expansion should be used when it could surface community, labor, neighborhood or public-history records unavailable through English-only discovery. Indigenous names, nations and source terminology must be used accurately and only with appropriate authority.

## Stable candidate IDs

Use `LV-[ZONE]-###` for Las Vegas Valley records, `LAS-[TERMINAL]-###` for airport records, `BC-HD-###` for Boulder City/Hoover Dam, and `NV-[PLACE]-###` for remote Nevada holds. Broad sites are source universes, not candidates. Each individual sign, clock, tunnel artwork, machine, marker, structure, installation, retained factory component or technical detail receives its own record.

Every record must contain all fields required by the controlling handoff, including geographic truth, access, current state, mechanism, hidden labor, causation classification, primary and independent evidence, actual visual, rights, RN capture, accessibility, content routes, duplication state and last-verification date.

## Integration rules

Every verified candidate receives one relationship state:

- independent;
- canonical prelude;
- canonical companion;
- canonical afterlife/update;
- event-linked;
- route cluster;
- reserve/do not publish yet.

Micro-place research cannot repair a weak canonical proposition by supplying a merely nearby object. Any relationship must be causal, documented continuation, contextual, concurrent, unresolved or rejected. A discovery may be excellent independently even when its canonical relationship is rejected.

The same actual visual cannot be silently reused as the principal asset for multiple packages. Rights, credits, captions and alt text remain item-level. Any audio version must explain the invisible or visual mechanism and use the standing RN real/verified-AI/hybrid disclosure system.

## RN burden controls

RN will not be sent to verify whether an object exists. Remote verification precedes field assignment. Each capture instruction must specify exact location, lawful viewpoint, access/ticket/security state, walking distance or transit burden, best timing, sensory conditions, shot list and nearby backup. Route clusters should minimize transfers and hand-intensive tasks. Permission-only or remote Nevada objects remain research holds unless their value justifies a separate RN decision.
