# Slot 13 — RN Ten-Frame Shot Card

**Only use after the production team confirms the exact product, retailer and lawful private photo location.**

## Bring

- charged phone
- small phone stand
- plain matte paper or cloth
- small ruler
- product and receipt
- companion help for opening, if useful

## Photograph

1. Receipt—item/store/date visible; cover payment details.
2. Sealed package front.
3. Sealed package back.
4. Compliance label close-up.
5. Package closure before opening.
6. Open package with every serving visible.
7. Every serving laid flat, marks facing up.
8. Typical mark close-up beside ruler.
9. Weakest or least clear mark close-up.
10. Package reclosed once.

## Stop and preserve

- Do not eat, cut or discard anything yet.
- If wrappers carry the marks instead of the food, photograph every wrapper front and back.
- Do not decide whether anything is compliant; just photograph what is there.
- Production handles filenames, records, captions, alt text, rights, redaction and QA.
