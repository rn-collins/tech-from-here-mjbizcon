## Purpose and limits

This package converts the remaining Nevada Cannabis Compliance Board (CCB) evidence gates into a narrow Nevada Public Records Act request and a deterministic retrieval matrix. It does not assert that the 2020–2021 Carson City hold was a Cannex event, that a particular GTI/Rise license held the inventory, that Greenway Case No. 2025-028 involved Bulletin 2025-02, or that any held product was released, remediated or destroyed.

The request must not be sent without RN's explicit authorization. Before sending, insert the requester's identity and contact information on the CCB form, confirm the fee cap, and conduct the legal/editorial review listed below.

## Verified submission rules

The CCB says public-record requests must be written and use its Public Records Request Form. It accepts them by email or mail, asks for specific requests and requester contact information, and states it will respond within five business days or provide written notice of the earliest reasonable production date. CCB also states that fees may reflect actual reproduction costs and will be disclosed before fulfillment. Nevada's public-record regulations contemplate electronic copies and require a written estimate when estimated actual reproduction cost exceeds $25.

## Classification and decision rules

Every returned record must be logged as **Confirmed**, **Disconfirmed**, **Unresolved** or **Superseded** with the exact source, date, page/field and identifier. Apply these rules:

1. **Cannex match:** Confirm only through an agency document expressly connecting the incidents or a shared license/product/package/batch identifier. Timing, city, corporate affiliation or retail-endpoint overlap is insufficient.
2. **License lineage:** Do not infer a legacy/current mapping from entity or address similarity. Require an agency-maintained crosswalk, license history or explicit agency confirmation.
3. **Law versus software:** A Metrc event is not itself a legal order. A legal order is not proof that the corresponding software status changed. Close the chain only when both are independently documented and temporally reconciled.
4. **Bulletin versus hold:** A bulletin, recall, advisory, quarantine, suspension and administrative hold are distinct unless the agency record links them.
5. **Disposition:** Absence from a later agenda, expiration of an order, reinstatement of a laboratory or amendment of a bulletin does not prove release, remediation, destruction or physical disposition.
6. **Quantities:** Do not substitute public list rows, identifiers, packages, units or sales for one another. Preserve the agency's original unit and reconcile opening, moved, sold, returned, remediated, destroyed, released and unresolved quantities.
7. **Version control:** Later policies or rule drafts do not establish earlier authority or practice. Record effective dates and label proposals as proposals.
