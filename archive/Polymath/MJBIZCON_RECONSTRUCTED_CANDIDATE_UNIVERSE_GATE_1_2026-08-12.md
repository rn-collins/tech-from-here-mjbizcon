# MJBizCon 2026 Canonical 14 — Reconstructed Candidate Universe

**Gate:** Phase 1 reconstruction after loss of the earlier 84-candidate file  
**Date:** 12 August 2026  
**Scope:** Nevada cannabis × technology × place  
**Count:** 84 candidates  
**Status:** Discovery universe only. No candidate is selected merely by appearing here.

## Why this reconstruction was necessary

The retroactive technology audit established that several inherited stories were technically defensible, but it did not prove that they were the most interesting, appealing, culturally resonant or publicly relevant stories available. The surviving slate also clustered around regulatory information systems. This reconstruction therefore treats every incumbent as a candidate, restores missing physical/product/cultural/labor/place pathways, and opens direct competition for all fourteen slots.

Evidence grades are preliminary:

- **A:** multiple strong public primary, legal or technical records already located.
- **B:** at least one strong authoritative trail plus an identifiable expansion route.
- **C:** compelling premise requiring substantial facility, community, worker or technical evidence.

Disposition labels are **advance**, **compare**, **hold**, or **reject at discovery**. “Advance” means only that the candidate merits Gate 2 comparison.

## Discovery-stage findings

### Candidates that immediately challenge weak incumbents

- **20 — The Sample That Reached the Laboratory Was Not the Product on the Shelf** has more conflict, human stakes and narrative movement than an abstract potency-distribution story.
- **23 — A Laboratory Could Inflate THC and Still Produce an Official-Looking Certificate** may be a stronger public story than either generic laboratory explanation or aggregate anomaly screening.
- **26 — The Kief-Enhanced Flower That Forced Nevada to Decide What the Product Was** restores a physical, current and visually compelling product technology.
- **50 — Nevada Makes Cannabis Waste Unrecognizable Before It Can Leave** restores material transformation, labor and environmental consequence.
- **58 — One Las Vegas Lounge Needs Negative Pressure. Another Uses the Desert Sky** may be the slate’s most distinctly Nevada place/technology comparison, but must be developed with tribal authority and review.
- **75 — A Nevada Cannabis Camera Must Be Available to Police in Real Time** is a more immediate surveillance story than a broad casino/cannabis institutional analogy.
- **84 — When a Cannabis Business Fails, Nevada Needs a Technical Plan for the Plants, Data and Keys** introduces business failure, ownership and operational continuity without duplicating routine tracking.

### Obvious ideas that remain demoted

- Constitutional legalization history remains a law/history asset rather than a central technology story.
- Generic desert sustainability remains held until facility-level Nevada water/energy/HVAC evidence exists.
- Generic blockchain, AI cultivation, robotics, QR-code and “smart dispensary” concepts were not admitted without a demonstrated Nevada genealogy or authoritative local evidence.
- The world’s-largest-bong/museum hook remains only a contender; spectacle alone does not establish consequence or a technology story.
- A government portal, checklist or form is not automatically a technology story merely because it is digital.
