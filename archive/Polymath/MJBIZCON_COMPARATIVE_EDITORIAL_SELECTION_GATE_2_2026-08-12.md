# MJBizCon 2026 Canonical 14 — Comparative Editorial Selection Gate 2

**Date:** 12 August 2026  
**Input:** reconstructed 84-candidate universe and all completed technology audits  
**Decision level:** provisional editorial selection; no article is drafting-cleared  
**Purpose:** select for public magnetism, cultural relevance and place-specific technological revelation—not merely evidentiary convenience.

## Controlling finding

The inherited slate was not the strongest possible fourteen. It overrepresented regulatory databases because public records made those propositions easier to prove. The reconstructed universe exposes stronger physical, cultural, product, labor, failure and jurisdiction stories.

This gate therefore removes protected-incumbent status. Existing prose, prior numbering and completed audits do not earn selection. The provisional slate below is a new editorial slate. Every newly promoted or materially combined proposition must still receive its own full four-lens technology audit and dossier before drafting.

## Scoring method and limitation

Candidates were compared on the controlling weighted rubric: revelation, source depth, location specificity, consequence, hidden-system explanatory power, local/outside novelty, human stakes, RN fit, visual richness, durability, saveability, within-series distinctiveness and feasible clearance. Scores are expressed on a 100-point scale.

These are **desk-based comparative editorial scores**, not measurements of audience preference. Fixed-query coding of 30–50 relevant exposure results per finalist and local cold-reader feedback remain required before the slate can be called final. The scores rank present evidence and editorial potential; they do not erase hard gates.

## Incumbent decisions

- **Story 3, potency-distribution enforcement lead:** demoted from the fourteen. Its strongest public functions are covered more vividly by the real laboratory-misconduct case and the measurement-chain story. Preserve as a reporting module or alternate if case correspondence transforms it.
- **Story 11, archival addressability:** demoted from the fourteen. It remains a valuable *Polymath* archive/history story but does not beat the selected slate on public urgency, visual mechanism or technological consequence.
- **Story 2:** retained narrowly because patient history and early medical infrastructure supply necessary chronological and human range. It remains at risk if the historical technology and patient experience cannot be made concrete.
- **Story 6:** retained conditionally. If the live audit produces no consequential or surprising findings, promote the laboratory-sampling case or ID-scanner story.
- **Story 7:** broad casino/cannabis analogy does not survive as selected. The narrower real-time camera-access proposition takes its place, with gaming used only where a documented comparison earns it.
- **Story 14:** retained but updated. The existence of listed licenses cannot substitute for public operating status; closure/pivot and current active operations must be accurately reported.

## Slate-balance audit

The provisional slate now includes:

- **people and access:** patient verification;
- **living/physical production:** plant identity and kief-enhanced flower;
- **consumer-facing information:** Item Catalog;
- **science and misconduct:** measurement chain plus laboratory manipulation;
- **public health and failure:** recall;
- **material labor/environment:** mandated waste destruction;
- **hospitality and business viability:** engineered lounge;
- **Indigenous sovereignty and design:** state indoor versus Paiute open-air consumption, plus cross-boundary data/test infrastructure;
- **surveillance/police access:** real-time cameras;
- **regulated-system limits:** illicit-market estimation;
- **race, geography and allocation:** social-equity classification and corrected rerun.

There is still a contemporary/regulatory tilt because the project is specifically Nevada cannabis × technology, but the selected mechanisms are no longer five versions of a database. The two Indigenous-centered stories require a strict nonduplication plan: Slot 10 owns consumption-space design and lived place; Slot 11 owns cross-jurisdictional product/test/data movement and sovereignty.

Geographic balance remains imperfect. Las Vegas/Clark County dominates because the event, lounge, regulator and market architecture concentrate there. The White Pine glasshouse and northern Nevada product/retail cases remain the strongest routes to broader geography and should be promoted if their evidence beats a conditional Las Vegas story.
