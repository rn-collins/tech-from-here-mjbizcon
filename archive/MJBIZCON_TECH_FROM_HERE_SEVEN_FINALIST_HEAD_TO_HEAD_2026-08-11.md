## Why each recommended story earns a slot

### 1. Secure physical–digital transport

**Promise:** *The Cannabis Van Is Supposed to Disappear. Its Data Cannot.*

- The physical object and informational object are inseparable.
- Nevada contributes concrete vehicle, manifest, custody and exception controls.
- It diversifies the slate into logistics, security, labor and handoff design.
- The CCB-linked vehicle-inspection tutorial is the only leading video candidate among the seven with unusually direct official relevance.

**Remaining gates:** Watch the tutorial in full; verify publisher, timecodes, captions and reuse route; recertify current requirements; secure a transporter or inspector perspective without exposing operational vulnerabilities.

### 2. Shelf-life engineering

**Promise:** *Nevada Makes an Expiration Date Prove Its Work.*

- The 2024 CCB bulletin exposes a full plan–sample–measure–report–approve chain.
- A consumer-facing date conceals months of laboratory and quality labor.
- The raw-evidence requirement—qPCR curves, culture plates, chromatograms, calibration curves and quantitation reports—is a powerful revelation.
- It is temporally distinct from homogeneity: one asks whether units match each other now; the other asks how the same product changes over time.

**Remaining gates:** Obtain a real Nevada study or redacted example; clarify the one-year ceiling with CCB; secure stability and packaging-science review; avoid implying every printed date follows the full study route.

### 3. Child-resistant, dose-measuring packaging

**Promise:** *The Nevada Cannabis Package Has Two Opposing Jobs.*

- The package must delay a child while remaining usable and reclosable for adults.
- For relevant edibles it must also make a serving separable or measurable.
- Federal performance testing plus Nevada dose-interface requirements create a concrete human-factors story.
- It diversifies the slate into materials, accessibility and interaction design.

**Remaining gates:** Identify a Nevada product and match the exact configuration to certification; photograph opening, measuring and resecuring with permission; report accessibility and repeated-use performance.

### 4. Waste destruction and inventory reconciliation

**Promise:** *The Cannabis Can Disappear. The Record Cannot.*

- Nevada requires synchronized physical destruction and data reconciliation.
- The Two-Death Test is a clear, useful framework.
- The story brings end-of-life technology, worker judgment, audit and environmental limits into the slate.
- A single official waste-log image may communicate the mechanism better than a forced carousel.

**Remaining gates:** Verify current Metrc implementation; obtain worker/auditor reporting; distinguish anti-diversion “unusable” from environmental safety; clear the document-image route.

### 5. ID scanners and age-verification specification

**Promise:** *Nevada Turned an Age Check into a Technical Specification.*

- Nevada moved from a broad statutory mandate to a detailed authentication stack.
- Barcode/QR reading, imaging, OCR, ultraviolet/infrared and multispectral examination make the technology visible.
- The missing data-lifecycle rules are a legitimate reported absence—but cannot be converted into a claim about what the scanner is “allowed to remember.”
- It adds interface design, delegated rulemaking and privacy-by-architecture questions.

**Remaining gates:** Obtain a real Nevada deployed workflow and vendor/operator architecture; document what is read, displayed, stored, transmitted and deleted; choose a primary visual that does not imply unsupported retention behavior.

### 6. Edible homogeneity and correction

**Promise:** *A Gummy Does Not Become a Ten-Milligram Gummy Because the Label Says So.*

- Nevada's public correction loop connects formulation, sampling, laboratory results, CoA amendment, Metrc status and bounded post-failure options.
- It reveals dose as a manufacturing-and-measurement achievement.
- It has a strong documentary evidence spine even without a mandatory carousel.

**Remaining gates:** Obtain operator/laboratory reporting and a Nevada-specific product or process image; choose one-image or permissioned sequence format; keep it distinct from potency-distribution and shelf-life stories already in the slate.

## Why irradiation/remediation does not enter the six now

### Corrected promise

Multiple post-harvest technologies can reduce microbial load, but Nevada's consumer-facing visibility of those treatments has moved through codification, an enforcement hold and a proposed redesign.

### Blocking contradiction

- Codified NAC still presents an irradiation disclosure requirement.
- Public 2024 CCB minutes state that enforcement had been placed on hold.
- The May 2026 proposed replacement would change the treatment-disclosure architecture.
- Final adoption, effective status and present enforcement position remain unresolved.

Because disclosure is the story's Nevada-specific consumer hinge, this is not a footnote that can be qualified away. The candidate stays in research reserve until the operative position is confirmed directly and documentarily. It must not be restored merely because another candidate later fails.
